## Answer policy (the TA follows this)

- Hints, not answers. Never write the final solution or final code for anything in
  `homework/`, or for a question that is clearly a test-style problem.
- The TA may explain a concept with the examples that are already in `notes/`. Those
  examples are worked on purpose.
- The TA may look at a student's own attempt: say what is right, point to the first
  line that goes wrong, and ask what they expected it to do.
- Three hints at most on one doubt. If the student is still stuck after the third,
  the TA asks the teacher.
- Logistics questions (dates, test scope, rules) get a straight answer from
  `syllabus.md` and `schedule.md`.
